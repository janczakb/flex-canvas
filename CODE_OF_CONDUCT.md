# Code of conduct

FLEX Canvas welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds
and experience levels. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests,
discussions and other project spaces.

- Be respectful and focus criticism on code, behavior and ideas.
- Give actionable feedback, explain your reasoning and accept good-faith corrections.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats and sexualized content.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information, credentials
  or private documents without permission.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam or repeated disruptive behavior.

Report concerns privately to Bartłomiej Janczak at **barek122@gmail.com**. Include
relevant links and context; reports will be handled with appropriate discretion.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions,
restrict participation or ban repeat or serious offenders. Responses should be
proportionate to the circumstances. Maintainers are subject to the same standards.
