# FLEX Canvas licensing

FLEX Canvas is source-available. You can install it from npm or load it from a
CDN; the same license applies to both.

## Free for your own operations

Personal, educational, research and internal company use is permitted without a
paid license. This includes applications a company builds to manage its own work,
products or services. Private evaluation is also free.

For example, an internal CRM, an inspection application used by your employees,
or document review for your own hotel business can qualify for free use. A
monetized software service offered to other companies is commercial product use.

## Included with your paid FLEX Fields license

**A valid paid FLEX Fields license automatically includes FLEX Canvas within that
license's scope, at no additional Canvas license charge.** This applies to existing
paid customers too. You can use Canvas on its own, without installing FLEX Fields,
Laravel or Filament.

The license you purchased still determines which products and entities are covered.
A Single Product license covers that product; an Unlimited Company license covers
that licensee's qualifying products. Enterprise coverage follows the accepted group
terms. Your existing scope, duration and update rights are preserved.

Commercial product use includes paid SaaS, monetized software platforms and software
services. Customer installations, SDK redistribution, OEM and reusable developer
products require express rights for that activity; an ordinary paid plan does not
add those rights automatically.

## Deploy the way your application needs

You may bundle or self-host the runtime, use the official npm/CDN distribution and
deliver the required JavaScript, CSS, WebAssembly, workers and fonts to browsers as
part of a permitted application. There is no required public badge or periodic
runtime activation. Keep the bundled license and third-party notices.

Your documents, images and annotation data remain yours. Browser delivery is not a
license for your users to redistribute the SDK as their own reusable product.
